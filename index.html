BORAPLAYER / INTERACTIVE PREVIEW

Your music. Your space.

Explore BoraPlayer’s interface with a fictional collection and original, generated instrumental samples. Add your own audio or video to try real local playback.

  1. Discover: search the sample collection and play a song.
  2. Library: save favorites and build a playlist.
  3. Now playing: tap the player, switch audio/video views and explore the queue. Audio keeps playing.
  4. Add local files: selected files stay in this tab. They are never uploaded and disappear when you reload.

In the installed app, supported features include YouTube Music discovery and playback, saving music, downloads, playlists, lyrics and optional account sync. These online features are described here but are not connected in this demo.

Advertising: all “Ad · Demo” areas are placement samples. No ad network, tracking, paid impressions or advertiser links are active. Final formats depend on provider approval.

No login, account access, live YouTube requests, conversions, server calls or analytics. GitHub Pages serves the static demo assets.
